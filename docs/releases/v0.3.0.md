## New in v0.3.0

- Nikon NEF/NRW Develop: native decoding, Vulkan processing, white balance, tone curves, HSL, detail and manual lens controls, local masks, presets and comparison views.
- Editable RAW layers retain their embedded camera source and settings in `.comp` projects. Reopen development by double-clicking the layer. Direct pixel edits require explicit, undoable rasterization.
- Direct 16-bit sRGB TIFF export from RAW Develop. File-menu TIFF and lossless WebP exports include the whole composition at 8-bit.
- Object-selection edge offset and geometric smoothing, plus remappable Tab switching between Wand and Object tools. The feature target is now macOS Compositor 1.1.8.

RAW camera support follows Rawler's RGB Bayer NEF/NRW decoders. The compositor remains 8-bit sRGB; lens corrections are manual and sensor-saturated highlights are not reconstructed. RAW metadata is a Linux project extension and may be discarded by other applications. See [RAW workflow and limits](https://github.com/AugusDogus/Compositor/blob/main/docs/linux-raw.md).
